# Mini-Synthesizer
While browsing videos I came across a pocket sized synthesizer that looked very fun to use, which immediately caught my eye. 
Upon further research, I was saddened by the price tag, thus the project was born. 
The aim for the product was to design a feature rich midi compatible and standalone device while having a simple and intuitive design that doesn’t require lots of knowledge to use.
This design is in no way meant to compete with features of the original product, just serve as a proof of concept of the capabilities DIY Tech has, drawing on inspiration from the 
UI and very basic features of the original.

The original product in question is the **Pocket Synth** by **Hichord**, as linked below
https://hichord.shop/
